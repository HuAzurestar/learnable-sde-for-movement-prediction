# NEX-380 OSM distance-field evidence

## Outcome and boundary

Two real-PBF interior smoke ranges produced all three channels at 50 m. These
are deliberately partial coverage artifacts, not claims of whole-GB or
whole-Zhejiang coverage. Every point outside the two masks remains unavailable
(`has_osm=0`, distance NaN). This delivery is a validated data pipeline and is
not training-ready; the complete five-item training integration gate is in
`docs/osm_distance_fields.md`.

| region | EPSG:4326 bounds | CRS | cap | covered pixels | immutable source key |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Zhejiang / Hangzhou | `[120.05, 30.15, 120.25, 30.35]` | EPSG:32651 | 5,000 m | 170,902 | `NEX-317-OSM-ZHEJIANG-20260813-A3001722` |
| GB / Westminster | `[-0.15, 51.49, -0.10, 51.53]` | EPSG:27700 | 2,000 m | 6,173 | `NEX-317-OSM-GB-20260813-E403B469` |

The immutable source registry is
`docs/data_registry/NEX-317-osm-snapshots.json`. It pins full SHA-256, byte
size, and PBF replication timestamp rather than treating a moving
`*-latest.osm.pbf` URL as a version.

## simple versus complete_ways acceptance evidence

Both Westminster extracts used the identical bbox
`[-0.20, 51.45, -0.05, 51.57]`, core bounds, CRS, 50 m resolution, 2 km cap,
coverage, and feature rules:

- `simple` PBF SHA-256:
  `5ca162c10579a971301d53da88a6f7a1f9a47346d457bbc48d750cba80325be0`;
- `complete_ways` PBF SHA-256:
  `72a4aa926a7b36aa0de4c2b4af48971640b9d3034a9b9a42e2b904cbefb0fcff`.

`scripts/compare_osm_distance_fields.py` exited `0`. All four GeoTIFFs were
byte-identical and each decoded array had zero differing pixels:

| layer | shared GeoTIFF SHA-256 | shared decoded-array SHA-256 | differing pixels |
|---|---|---|---:|
| road | `0101fb06...0384c` | `045a658f...c5d0` | 0 |
| water | `50660de7...67e3f` | `88a47225...4132` | 0 |
| building | `3d19b7ef...ceba` | `e9d13752...5e29` | 0 |
| coverage | `af6b8f06...f012` | `ab0c2c7c...578e` | 0 |

Therefore the Westminster `simple` degradation is accepted solely by exact
same-config comparison against `complete_ways`; boundary margin is not used as
a substitute proof. The complete machine-readable result is
`artifacts/nex380/gb_westminster_simple_vs_complete.json`.

The validation slice
`10177005_Freestyle_2023_09_13_17_10_03_cond.parquet` contains 4,821 rows. The
non-mutating adapter produced 2,345 covered and 2,476 uncovered rows, preserved

## Failures and publication safety

The formal record is `RC-NEX380-20260827-001` at
`docs/rc_failure_registry/NEX-380-RC-001.md`. It contains commands, original
errors/statuses and elapsed evidence, impact/no-output declarations,
workarounds, and verification for missing system osmium/GDAL, the 600 s
`complete_ways` gate, and both adapter failures.

Raster builds and augmented parquet trees now publish only after completing in
a sibling temporary directory. CRS must be projected with metre axes; coverage
must be valid Polygon/MultiPolygon geometry; overlapping manifests and any
non-finite covered OSM value fail honestly.
