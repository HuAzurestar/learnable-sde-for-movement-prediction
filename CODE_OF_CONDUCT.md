# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, technically rigorous, and harassment-free
project environment.

Contributors should:

- discuss ideas and code without attacking people;
- distinguish verified results from hypotheses and incomplete work;
- disclose limitations, failed experiments, and conflicts of interest;
- avoid publishing personal information, private data, or confidential material;
- give credit for code, proofs, datasets, and research contributions.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict
participation when behavior threatens contributor safety, research integrity, or
data confidentiality. Report sensitive conduct concerns through a private
maintainer channel rather than a public issue.
